Most SX1262 boards transmit at 22 dBm. Clutter adds height to the terrain for trees or buildings the elevation data leaves out; it is not applied within 50 m (160 ft) of either end. Extra loss is subtracted from every link margin, so you can calibrate against links you know work. k = 4/3 models normal atmospheric refraction.
